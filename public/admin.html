Image Storage & Persistence Guide

To ensure slider images uploaded from your phone or computer remain permanently stored across server updates and redeployments, our system supports both Cloudinary Cloud Storage and Railway Persistent Volumes.

Option 1: Cloudinary (Recommended for Railway - Free)

1. Create a free account at cloudinary.com.
2. Copy your Cloud Name, API Key, and API Secret from your dashboard.
3. Add these variables in Railway → Your Project → Variables: 

CLOUDINARY_CLOUD_NAME=your_cloud_name
CLOUDINARY_API_KEY=your_api_key
CLOUDINARY_API_SECRET=your_api_secret
Option 2: Railway Persistent Volume

If you have attached a persistent volume to your Railway service (mounted at /database), set: 

UPLOADS_PATH=/database/uploads/slider
